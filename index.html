Teacher Bio

Hi! I’m Mr. M. I help students become thoughtful creators with technology. My classroom focuses on problem-solving, collaboration, and creativity, giving every student a chance to practice real-world tech skills in a supportive environment.

Class Description

Students learn the foundations of computer science and digital making. Projects include block coding, beginning web development, multimedia production, and design challenges that connect to everyday life. We emphasize responsible technology use, growth mindset, and teamwork.
